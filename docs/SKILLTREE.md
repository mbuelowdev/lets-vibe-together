# Skilltree

There are X skill trees. The game starts with the YYYY skill tree the only one being unlockable, the others are still
hidden. They unlock through story events.

Regarding what applies to what:
So there are two entites: The player and the workers. Modifiers either apply to the player, the workers or to both. In
the starter tree basically everything applies to the player. As the game progresses more and more are worker and global
modifiers.

## General Tree (1st) (Starter Tree)

- Buy a scam artist book (higher success chances) (start node)
- Buy an English book to improve your odds (higher success chance)
- Buy tragedy book to recite 1/2/3 (higher success chance)
- Buy vodka to play for longer (more target gain)
- Buy intel from internet "friends" 1/2/3 (more target gain)
- Buy a pro account for the website you sell your skins (more money per skin)
- Buy expensive skins to increase your scamming margin 1/2/3 (more money per skin)

## Automation Tree (2nd)

- Teach workers how to better identify targets 1/2/3 (higher success chance) (start node)
- Teach workers how to manipulate the market 1/2/3 (sold skins sell for more) (start node)
- Teach workers how to succeed scam directly (workers can now generate skins) (start node)
- Teach workers how to sell skins for profit (workers can now sell skins directly)
- Buy a book about people skills 1/2/3 (workers trigger faster)
- Buy a book managing people (workers trigger faster)
- Hire someone from the street 1/2/3 (adds workers)
