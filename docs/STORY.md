# Story

The protagonist is a 21 year old jobless russian man who lives with his mother (who is a whore) in a tower block. They
constantly lack money and they do everything, including crime, to stay alive. In this story the man discovers that he
can scam people online and steal their cs skins to make money. He starts off by scamming individuals, then proceeds to
invite other people to assist his scamming. His scamming operation grows and grows until he branches into scamming
through other means like fake bills or fake phone calls. The story ends with the government giving him a job to
scam western european countries to destabilize them.

In the first part of the story the protagonist chats with his old friend about how to do it, how to get to the people
that have skins (this explains the basic game loop). The protagonist then repeats the loop to get enough money to pay
his part of the rent this month. Once he realizes that this really works he wants to scam more people (this unlocks the
first skill tree). He gets better at scamming and finding targets. He started hiring people from the streets with a
computer. (Unlocking the last node in the first skill tree introduces automation and unlocks the 2nd skill tree).

(By unlocking the 2nd skill tree):
The second part of the game focuses on extending the automation. First it is just hired help in finding targets, then he
also teaches people how to scam. His business grows. He eventually hires a shady software guy that helps him automate
his scamming with software (unlocks the 3rd skill tree).
(Unlocking room planner software? He buys an office and hires people?)

(By unlocking the 4th skill tree):
The third part of the story focuses on him branching into new scamming areas. He unlocks phone and printer software to
scam people via phone and letters.
(Also extended office building?)

The story ends when the player reaches a million ruble with a phone call from the government offering him to target his
scams.
